# David Hair Factory Men's & Women's Hair System Quote Calculation Formulas & Price List (AUD Only)

This document sets out the complete pricing architecture for **David Hair Factory Custom Orders and Stock Systems across both Men's Hair Systems and Women's Hairpieces (Toppers & Full Cap Wigs)**. All prices are converted exclusively into **Australian Dollars (AUD)** based on the exchange rate as of **12 November 2026 (`1 USD = 1.4632 AUD`)**, allowing you to plug figures directly into the formulas below for instant quotation.

---

## 1. Core Calculation Formulas (AUD)

### 1.1 Stock Systems (Men's Toupees & Women's Toppers / Wigs)

$$\text{Final Price (AUD)} = \text{Model Base Price} + \text{Base Cutting (Optional: AUD \$21.95)} + \text{Hair Cut-in \& Styling (Optional: AUD \$58.53)}$$

### 1.2 Custom Orders (Cumulative Formula for Both Men's & Women's Units)

For custom-made orders, all fixed add-ons (base size/construction, hair length, and special processes) are first added to the starting base price to obtain the **Subtotal ($S$)**, which is then multiplied by the combined percentage surcharges for **Hair Quality** and **Hair Density**.

$$\text{Subtotal }(S) = \text{Starting Base Price} + \text{Base Size/Construction Add-on} + \text{Hair Length Add-on} + \sum(\text{Other Fixed Options})$$

$$\text{Final Price (AUD)} = S \times (1 + \text{Hair Quality Surcharge Rate} + \text{Density Surcharge Rate})$$

### 3.3 Hair Quality & Density Percentage Multipliers

Multiply the fixed subtotal ($S$) by the sum of the applicable percentage rates below:

* **Hair Quality (Raw Hair Grade):**
  * Standard Indian Human Hair: **+0%** (`0.00`)
  * Remy Human Hair: **+40%** (`0.40`)
  * European Hair: **+40%** (`0.40`)
  * Premium Virgin Hair: **+70%** (`0.70`)

* **Hair Density:**
  * Light \~ Medium-Light (60% \~ 100%): **+0%** (`0.00`)
  * **Medium (110%) when combined with Hair Length $\ge 12"$:** **+15%** (`0.15`) *(Automatic long-hair density surcharge)*
  * Medium-Heavy (120% \~ 130%): **+25%** (`0.25`)
  * Heavy (140% \~ 150%+): **+35%** (`0.35`)
